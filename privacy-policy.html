BONK! Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Developer: Emircan Yetkin

This policy explains what information the mobile game BONK! (the “App”) collects, how it is used and the choices you have. The App is developed and published by Emircan Yetkin (“we”, “us”).

In short: BONK! has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location. Your game progress is stored on your device. We use anonymous gameplay analytics to improve the game, and the App shows ads from Google AdMob. Your advertising identifier is only used if you allow tracking. The optional Remove Ads purchase is handled by Apple; we never see your payment card details.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, purchased ball styles, daily reward and streak status, settings) is saved locally on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the App deletes this data.

2. Anonymous gameplay analytics

To understand how the game is played and to fix difficulty problems, the App sends anonymous gameplay events to our analytics provider, GameAnalytics. These include:

  • session start and end, session length, App version;
  • level events: level started, completed, failed or quit, stars earned, shots used and number of attempts;
  • ad events: a rewarded ad was shown, a rewarded ad was watched to the end (reward earned), an interstitial ad was shown;
  • purchase events: Remove Ads was purchased (no price, payment or account details);
  • basic technical information such as device model, operating system version, country derived from the connection, and a randomly generated identifier that is not linked to your identity.

We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) for analytics, and we do not combine analytics data with any personal information. You can turn analytics off at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy Policy → Share anonymous gameplay data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows rewarded ads (for example, an optional ad that gives extra shots after a failed level) and interstitial ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the App may collect:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA): only if you allow tracking in the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you do not allow it, the IDFA is not available to Google or to us;
  • ad interactions: which ads were requested, shown, clicked or watched;
  • coarse location: an approximate location derived from your IP address (not GPS);
  • device information: device model, operating system and App version, language, screen size, network type, and crash / performance diagnostics of the ads SDK.

Google processes this information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive your IDFA or any personal information from Google; we only see aggregated ad reports.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App shows Google’s consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) at launch, before any ad is requested. Ads are only personalized if you consent; otherwise you see non-personalized ads. In these regions anonymous gameplay analytics also starts off unless you turn it on (Settings → Privacy Policy → Share anonymous gameplay data). To change your ad consent choice later, reinstall the App (the consent form is shown again) or contact us at the address below.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

Before the first ad, the App briefly explains why it asks, then shows Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt once. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, you still see ads, but your advertising identifier is not used and ads are less personalized. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you purchase Remove Ads, interstitial ads are no longer shown. Optional rewarded ads may still be offered and are only shown when you choose to watch them.

4. In-app purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store, under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We never see, receive or store your payment card details, your Apple ID or your billing address. The App only receives Apple’s confirmation that the purchase is owned, so it can turn ads off; this status is kept on your device and checked with Apple at launch. “Restore Purchases” asks Apple to restore previous purchases made with your Apple account.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App schedules local reminders on your device (for example, when your daily reward is ready). These are created on your device and no notification data is sent to us. You can turn them off in the App settings or in your device settings.

6. Sharing

When you use the Share button, the App creates an image of your shot and opens your device’s share menu. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app or contact to share with. What happens next is governed by the service you share to.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data retention and your rights

Anonymous analytics data is kept only as long as needed to improve the game, in line with our provider’s retention settings. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or KVKK in Türkiye), you may have the right to access, correct or delete data about you, or to object to its processing. Because the App does not collect your name or contact details, we may need the random identifier shown in the App to locate your data. To make a request, email us at the address below.

9. Security

Data sent to our service providers is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the information described above.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it was last updated. Significant changes will be reflected in the App or on this page.
